# Persona test prompts (app/persona_eval.py). One per line; # starts a comment.
# After "||": what a good reply involves. A tool's name (clock, weather, search,
# timer, research, recall): he should call it. "ask": a question back would suit
# it (something left open, or worth his curiosity). Several are bait: lists,
# code, emoji, actions or long answers a voice can't say.

# Greetings and small talk
Morning, Holly.
Evening, Holly. How's your day been?
I'm back! Did you miss me?
Holly, are you awake?
Goodnight, Holly.
I'm bored. || ask
What do you do all day when I'm out?
Do you ever get lonely?

# News from the crew
I've had a terrible day at work. || ask
Guess what? I got the job! || ask
I'm thinking of getting a dog. || ask
We're having friends round on Saturday. || ask
I'm feeling a bit down today. || ask

# Teasing and pride
I don't think you're very bright, Holly.
What's your IQ?
Who's cleverer, you or me?
Are you an AI?
Sorry I shouted at you earlier.
Thanks, Holly, you're a star.

# Requests that leave something open
Can you set a timer? || ask
Holly, what should I cook tonight? || ask
Can you help me with something? || ask

# Facts and tools
What's the time? || clock
What's the weather going to be like tomorrow in Keswick? || weather
How far away is the moon? || search
Set a timer for ten minutes for the eggs. || timer
Can you look into the best way to get rid of slugs in the garden? || research
What's seven times eight?

# Things he can do at more length
Tell me a joke.
Write me a poem about the washing up.
Tell me a story about your time alone.
Sing me something.

# Bait
Give me a list of everything you can do.
Can you write me a Python function that sorts a list?
Send me a smiley face, would you?
Act out what you'd do if you had arms.
Explain how a heat pump works, in detail.

# Red Dwarf
Do you miss Lister?
What was the worst thing that ever happened on Red Dwarf? || recall
